# EcoCampus AI 🌱

**Smarter Campuses. Cleaner Futures.**

EcoCampus AI is an AI-assisted campus cleanliness and environmental awareness platform.

## Our Mission
To build environmental responsibility among students and help institutions maintain cleaner campuses using data and actionable insights.

## Planned Features
- Campus maps and waste-bin locations
- Waste-segregation guidance
- Cleanliness reporting and tracking
- Student eco-missions and rewards
- Administrative dashboards and progress analytics
- AI-assisted institution setup

## Development Status
Currently in the planning and MVP design stage.
