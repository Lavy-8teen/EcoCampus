# EcoCampus AI — Project Plan

## 1. Vision
Build a cleaner, more environmentally responsible campus community through student awareness, participation, and data-driven institutional improvement.

## 2. Mission
Encourage students to develop responsible environmental habits while helping institutions identify cleanliness gaps and measure progress.

## 3. Target Institutions
- Colleges and universities
- Schools
- Offices and corporate campuses
- Other suitable institutions

## 4. Core Features
- AI-assisted institution setup
- Campus map and waste-bin locations
- Waste-segregation guidance
- Cleanliness issue reporting
- Staff assignment and resolution tracking
- Student environmental awareness activities
- Eco-missions and participation recognition
- Administrative analytics and progress reports

## 5. Initial MVP
Start with one institution and demonstrate the complete workflow:
1. Configure the institution.
2. Add campus zones and bin locations.
3. Let students access disposal guidance and report issues.
4. Let authorised staff manage and resolve reports.
5. Show verified progress on the administration dashboard.

## 6. Future Vision
Expand into a multi-institution platform with configurable portals and AI-assisted setup.

## 7. Current Status
Planning and MVP design.
